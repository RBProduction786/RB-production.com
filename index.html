Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a project take?

A short promo can take one to two weeks. Brand films and 3D animation usually take four to eight weeks. We confirm a timeline after the brief.

What do I need to start?

A short description of your goal, audience, deadline and budget. We handle script and planning from there.

Can you work with footage I already have?

Yes. We edit, grade and add motion graphics or VFX to existing footage.

What formats do you deliver?

4K and HD masters, plus cut-downs for YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and broadcast.

How many revisions are included?

We agree the number of revision rounds in the quote, so there are no surprises.
